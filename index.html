Seen

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026
Contact: pyantasy+support@gmail.com

What Seen is

Seen is an iOS app for tracking which TV episodes you have watched and rating episodes on a 1–10 scale. Show and episode metadata comes from a shared cache powered by TVmaze (used under CC BY-SA).

Data we store on your device

In the current version of Seen:

  • Watch progress (which episodes you marked watched)
  • Episode ratings (or “skipped” / unrated)

This information stays on your device using on-device storage (SwiftData). It is not uploaded to our servers for sync or backup in this version.

Accounts

The current App Store version does not offer user accounts or cloud sync of watch progress. Optional account code may exist in the product for a future release but is turned off for this version.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Seen shows banner ads on some list screens (Watching, Upcoming, Watchlist, and Library). We use Google AdMob to display ads.

AdMob and related Google advertising services may collect or process device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and interaction data for ad delivery and measurement. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choices in the App Tracking Transparency prompt and system settings.

For more about Google’s practices, see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob documentation.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch (and as required by Apple), Seen may ask for permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising. You can allow or deny this request. You can change related settings later in iOS Settings.

What we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. Watch history and ratings stored on your device are not sold.

Third-party data sources

Show titles, episode lists, posters, and related metadata are obtained via our backend from TVmaze. See TVmaze’s licensing and attribution terms. An attribution link is also available in the app Settings.

App Store privacy labels

For App Store Connect nutrition labels in this version, expect disclosures consistent with:

  • Advertising data (AdMob)
  • On-device watch / rating data (not collected by us for cloud accounts in this version)

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: pyantasy+support@gmail.com.
